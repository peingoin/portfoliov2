## The problem

Planning a trip with one transfer took nine taps in the existing transit app, and the live delay information was hidden two screens deep.

## What I did

I redesigned the journey planner around one question: when do I need to leave?

- Shadowed five commuters and mapped where they got stuck.
- Rebuilt the flow so a saved trip takes two taps.
- Ran an unmoderated test with 20 people and iterated twice.

## Result

Task time for planning a saved trip fell from 41 seconds to 12 in testing.

## What I learned

Watching people use the old app taught me more in one morning than a week of looking at other apps.
