## The problem

Study advice online was either an hour long or too thin to use.

## What I did

I wrote, shot and edited 12 videos of 40 seconds each. Every one covers a single technique with one example.

- Wrote all scripts before shooting, at 95 words each.
- Built a caption and title template to keep the edit fast.

## Result

The series reached 48,000 views in its first month. The video on spaced repetition accounts for a third of that.

## What I learned

Cutting a script from 150 words to 95 improved every video.
