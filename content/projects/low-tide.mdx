## The problem

A stretch of shoreline near my home is busy every morning before sunrise, and almost nobody in the city knows why.

## What I did

I spent five weeks filming three foragers on nine mornings, then cut 22 hours of footage down to 12 minutes.

- Recorded interviews separately so the shoreline scenes could run without talking heads.
- Mixed the sound myself, using field recordings from each location.

## Result

The film screened at a student festival and was shortlisted for best documentary.

## What I learned

The edit only worked once I removed my own narration.
