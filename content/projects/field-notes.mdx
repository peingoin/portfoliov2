## The problem

The hiking club had 60 members and no consistent look. Every poster was made from scratch.

## What I did

I designed a small identity system: a logo, two typefaces, a colour set and poster templates anyone in the club could fill in.

- Presented three directions to the committee.
- Built the templates in Figma so non-designers could use them.

## Result

The club has used the templates for every event since. Sign-ups for the first trip of the year rose from 18 to 31.

## What I learned

A system other people can use is worth more than one perfect poster.
