## The problem

The school timetable was a PDF. Finding the next room meant zooming into a grid on a phone between classes.

## What I did

I built a web app that opens straight to the next lesson. It reads the timetable once, stores it on the device, and works without a signal in the corridors.

- Interviewed 12 students about how they checked their schedule.
- Prototyped three layouts and tested them in the five minutes between lessons.
- Built the final version in React with offline storage.

## Result

140 students used it in the first term. The most common feedback was that it opened faster than the PDF.

## What I learned

Speed was the feature. Every extra tap I removed mattered more than anything I added.
